# Decision 0004: Keep the fraud-readiness claim bounded to evidence

Date: 2026-08-18

Status: accepted

## Context

The workbench demonstrates temporal validation, calibration, capacity-constrained policy analysis, sensitivity testing, monitoring, and governed refusal. Those methods transfer to fraud strategy. The source outcome is first-EMI default, however, and the data does not provide confirmed fraud, fraud loss, channel and device events, entity links, case outcomes, or investigator dispositions.

## Decision

Describe the current project as a strong credit-risk strategy product with fraud-transferable methods. Do not describe it as a fraud model, fraud control system, or evidence of end-to-end fraud operations.

Rate current Director of Fraud Analytics readiness at 4.5 of 10 using the published six-dimension rubric. Require owner-approved real fraud data and operating evidence before advancing the fraud-specific data, strategy, investigator, rollout, monitoring, and operating-pack milestones. Synthetic labels do not satisfy the data gate.

## Why

Transferable methods are not equivalent to evidence of fraud outcomes or control ownership. The claim boundary must follow the source definition and the operating evidence actually present.

## Alternatives rejected

- Rebrand first-EMI default as fraud, because the label does not establish intent or confirmed loss.
- Create synthetic fraud labels, because they would manufacture the evidence required by the claim.
- Treat model lift alone as Director readiness, because the role also owns economics, customer friction, operations, controls, rollout, and governance.

## Not done

No fraud label, fraud loss, identity graph, case outcome, investigator result, production control, or operating performance was invented. No external dataset was acquired.

## Changed

The public roadmap now records a six-dimension scorecard, explicit non-claims, and ten promotion-gated milestones from claim boundary through the Director operating pack.

## Consequences

The project can improve analytical communication and governance locally, but visual polish cannot close the fraud-data gap. The roadmap target of at least 8 of 10 remains conditional on completing the fraud-specific milestones with real approved evidence.
